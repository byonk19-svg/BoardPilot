# Woodworking Planner

register: product

One person plans plywood furniture at a desk in normal daylight, then reads printed dimensions at a workbench. The interface is a calm practical design tool, with a large model, legible measured dimensions and a direct manual workflow. Warm neutral surfaces, dark readable type and one deep green selection/action color support inspection. Technical statuses identify actual dimensions checked, missing construction method and the current revision. Never imply structural approval. Avoid a marketing hero, decorative dashboards, tiny labels, nested cards or AI chat as the primary workflow. Manual editing remains useful without AI or a viewport gesture.
